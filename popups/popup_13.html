"THE LOW PROFILE COLLECTION"

2025 - 2026

The Low Profile Collection was a secretive community update for "Team Fortress 2" centered around the Spytech theme. It released with a trailer, 10 maps, countless of cosmetics, paints and the Rush Gamemode as a collaboration inclusion. It launched with a trailer, and a website that showcased the content of the update

I was recruited to the project sometime after i made the map Flowerpot to showcase the Rush Gamemode, and we're told to keep the project in absolute secrecy up to the announcement of the project. This was done for many factors, but one of them was to keep an inline development team that could work on the project without community disturbanse

My role for the project was a Coder, Mapper and Gamemode Manager for Rush and other gamemodes that might be made. With that i set togheter a temporary discord to organize a team of developers who would be developing 4 Rush maps for the project

Over the span of a year i would work togheter with more than 60 people from things like the projects promotional material, the projects trailer, and by helping recruit more developers to the development of the project

As per requested they wished me to create a map for the project, shortly after joining i begun the map i would create development. Later on in the near of the end of the project we decided upon that each map should have a trailer, i volunteered to create all 10 of them, and we're able to do so after much work. Unfortently, in the end none of them we're used for the website

"MONOCEROS"

2025 - 2026

Monoceros is the second Rush map made by me the creator of the gamemode, and the first Rush map for the Low Profile Project.
It is a map set in a canyon somewhere in America about where the BLU team infiltrates a massive facility to blow up its infastrcture, then for them to move onto the back of the facility where a backyard town lies

The map had a rought start where it did not play out correctly, the first version was initially made to try to make it so the areas around the objectives would be destructible, which on the top of me having to design the map around a Spytech style, made it difficult to adjust for paths and flank routes

After accounting for the feedback which we're too negative to ignore on multiple servers, i completely redesigned the map and made it into what the map would be known for today

Monoceros released in the Summer of 2026 as with the rest of the Project maps to positive receptions, from both the players and the playtesters about its gameplay and its visuals

That said, i wasn't only asked to create a singular map, as the creator of the Rush gamemode i was the Project mananger who guided people to make more Rush maps for the gamemode and the projects goal. During the duration of the project, i would be leading a team of 21 developers, whom 5 of them we're mapmakers who would be making the maps for the gamemode and the project.

My role in this was mostly to just organize people around where i hosted playtests and mentored those who we're making the map's, such as rooting out bugs or on how they should design their maps

"MENTOR - MAINFRAME"

2025 - 2026

Mainframe was supposed to be the third Rush map made by another team of developers for Rush.
It is set in a robot factory, centered around where BLU has to do several objectives, including Rush one's

I was supposed to implement the Rush mechanics, by the request of the author of the map who wished me to have a role in the maps development.
Mainframe was not supposed to be a Rush map at first, it was originally going to be a Payload map with capture point elements, but after me and the project leader came to a conclusion for how many maps we would need, the creator of Mainframe reached out to me and requested the map to be in the Rush gamemode.

Mainframe was not able to be completed in time, so in the end the map author and the group behind it decided that i was to be pushed later for 2027

"PRE & POST LAUNCH"

2026

During the projects development, the owner of this project had quite some grand ambitious that they hoped for when gathering developers for the project, this was after all their last project for Team Fortess 2 after having hosted many through the years, alongside how i was going to end my career for TF2

A few demands for quality was asked early on, like more voice actors, while the maps for the most part was developed, we came into trouble at the very end of the project with the voice actors some two weeks prior to the release of the project, the hoster of the project insisted we would implement the "two actor system", which is where there is two actors playing as attakcing and defending roles

I had to push the actors to get most things done, but in the end we we're able to meet all of the actor goals that we're question

The second thing planned early on, was short but effctive custom soundtracks that would be used as sound cues for the Rush gamemode. Already as early as September 2025 had i asked the music artist to create some, but due to delays and bad planning, the music was only completed until late March of 2026. We we're however the same as the actors, able to complete the soundtracks just in the nick of time for release

Something that was not planned initially was map trailers for each of the maps for the project, this came about after i had completed my own map, and i said i would volunteer to create all of the trailers for the maps. Often i could create them as i pleased, but somethimes trailers became to create, as i often had to create one or two trailers each single day per the map to accompany the amount that was needed to be placed on the website

I was able to create all 10 videos for most of the maps that was not already made trailers for by other mappers, but unfortently due to cuts in the sites development, none of the trailers was used in the Website for the projects showcase. Which in a way, made these trailers redundant

Something that was also not initially planned was the Rush trailer. While the main trailer would serve as the main introduction to the gamemode i had created and the project overall, the project leader insisted we also had to have a trailer for the mode, stating it would not much sense if we had a whole page for this gamemode with no trailer. The trailer was rushed (pun intended) where we had to throw togheter all the actors to record for it and prayed for the lines, while a writer had to write them rather quickly for it take shape. I also had to step in a lot of times to write the script in order for it work

Alongside the map trailers, i was also tasked to make most of the shots of the trailer for the Main project trailer, per the trailer linked here in most of the shots of the maps, i made those

But just like the other things i was asked to make, the Rush was also created, which helped to boost the interest for Rush a little bit

In the end we we're able to meet most of our goals that we had set for ourself for this project. 5 Rush maps we're planned for this project, 2 was cut where 1 never really started development, 1 was supposed to play like the map "Steel" where certain objectives on the map could be destroed before the other, while 1 was delayed to next year (Mainframe)

The Low Profile Collection released to excepted receptions, where it was mixed where at a time of its release, Valve had just promised a new Mann VS Machine update, and at a time where the community was at its all time low, from overtely toxic behaviour

Its hard to say what came off from the Profile update, i even met with some of the developers to discuss the update. Only one item from the collection was added, so its not exactly a victory so to say. But one thing i am sure of, is that i felt enjoyed to have been a part of it, and had learned something from it
