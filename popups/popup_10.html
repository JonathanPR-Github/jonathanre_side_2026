"GULLYPASS COLLECTION"

2025

For a while during the creation of my last deathrun map, i had in mind to create something based upon an area that i could see from driving home from the Cabin, that was a steep slope between two mountains where it went down for miles with forest ending only in a lake at the bottom and then going right back up another mountain

The idea still lingers with me even now for a potential level for a game, but i knew that i had to try to make it come to vision

This was around the time i came in contact with the greater TF2 community when i was introduced by the Artist that made the logo's for my map's at that time

"GULLYPASS"

2025

Gullypass was that idea, and it was designed in a way where both teams would start on a road from eachother between the two slopes, where they had to run down that slope to reach a cabin in the middle of the map, that would contain the objective being the control point

The map is set in a said fjord somewhere in Norway where both the RED and BLU team attack a Radio Station broadcasting some messages that they intend to control. The map is surrounded with a huge walley that goes for miles along outside the maps boundries.

Gradually as i developed it went from first just being a simple HUT in the middle of the map, to being a radio station to give some proper meaning to attacking the map

Gullypass released to mixed receptions, while it was praised for its visuals and the playtesters did enjoy it, the overall vision of the map did seem to resenoate with players that much unfortently due to some lighting factors and the map's overall design

"GULLYCREAK"

2025

Not long after having developed Gullypass, i sat on too create the Halloween varient of the map, for the halloween session update that same year

Gullycreak would be made different, by that a portal would occasionally above the control point in the radio tower that had been ripped apart, that when entered would send you the map's underworld, where you'd need to traverse a obstacle course in order to escape the underworld. That and the new style of the map that would match the Halloween feeling

This was also the map when i begun working with an Artist who would be creating the promotional material for the map, such as its logo and steam frames. Thanks to them i was able to promote the map better, and we had a solid working reliation to when i stopped working on the Source Engine

Gullycreak was also released to mixed receptions, while it was praised for its visuals and the playtesters did enjoy it, the overall vision of the map did seem to resenoate with players that much unfortently due to some lighting factors and the map's overall design
