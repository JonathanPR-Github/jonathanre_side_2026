"THE HOI4 JOHN COLLECTION"

2024 - ONGOING

The HOI4 JOHN Collection is a collection 31 Mods for the game "Heart's of Iron 4" that i developed within a span of a year

Developed in the bracket language used by Paradox games, it initially started as a few grapichal mods but then gradually became more advanced mods adding functionalities to the game as time passed on

The Most famous mod in the collection is the "Minimalistic Battle Plans" mod, that has over 1.000 Ratings, 50.000 subscribers and over 220,500 views on Steam!

Other notable mods in the collection is the "OWB: Special Projects" mod, adding technologies to the Total overhaul mod "Old World Blues", and "Expanded Helicopter Technologies" that adds expanded technolgies to Vanilla HOI4

Throughout the mods development i learned a lot of coding with the Paradox Bracket language and further insight within game grapichs. The collection also eventually led me to become a developer within the Total Conversation mod "Red Dusk"
