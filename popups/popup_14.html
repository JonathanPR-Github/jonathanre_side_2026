"VIPRACE GAMEMODE"

2026

Sometime after I developed Monoceros, I was frantically messaged by one of the directing writers of the mode "Mobster VIP" for TF2, who asked me to step in for a role as coder as the leading coder for the said mode, could not step in to make the request

VIP is a gamemode about escorting a character that usually is pretty defenceless, across a map to the very end of it, where upon reaching the end of it the round is won for those who were escorting the VIP, while the other team who were supposed to kill the VIP will lose.
VIP was scrapped for Team Fortress 2 during its development between 1996 and 2007 where it eventually became the gamemode Payload.
When VScript was introduced to TF2 in 2022, VIP was recreated for TF2 reimagined in a new way that would make it fun dubbed "Mobster VIP", hence the character who is a Mobster. They gave the VIP a weapon and had him be more a supportive/attacking role

I was asked to recreate VIPR, which is a new spin on the VIP gamemode that was introduced by the standalone game "Team Fortress 2: Classified", in which there were two VIPs instead of 1, that would fight to combat over territory, instead of being escorted.

Having learned the full potential of using AI to code backend code for you, I spent a full two weeks coding the gamemode in ChatGPT then later Claude and rubberducking the code to see if it would work, creating over 5 variants of it based upon the requests I was getting from the said writer, and then ending up with a version that would work on the said 2 week mark

Something that would otherwise have taken 5 months, was done in under 2 weeks. Having completed the code, I continued to refine it, and made temporary models that could be swapped out for new custom ones, setting up the code necessary for the modellers to take over, who would take the most time to finish the gamemode

To test VIPR, we used the Valve maps Powerhouse and Viaduct, which was requested by those from the VIP Mobster team, that I edited to have the VIPR gamemode in it

Over the course of a few months we would continue to refine the gamemode and the abilities of the characters, until the models and maps necessary was done for the modes original vision to be completed

VIPR was received positively, with most people saying it was quite a fun spin for a reimagination of the gamemode

However by the time most of the content had been built, the development of this gamemode to drain much of my energy, seeing as I was developing this solely on my own. As with my plans to end TF2 modding, I decided to end the development of the project and to focus on other more important matters, which at the time was the Low Profile Project
