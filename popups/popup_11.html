"THE RUSH GAMEMODE"

2025 - 2026

Being a vivid enjoyer of the Battlefield games, I came across an idea sometime during the creation of Gullypass about that that I could try to make a gamemode where it would play exactly like it would be with Battlefield's Rush Gamemode

Rush is a gamemode from the Battlefield: Bad Company where one team attacks and the other defends a series of objectives. Attackers must plant and detonate explosives on designated targets, while defenders try to stop them by disarming charges or eliminating the opposing team. As objectives are destroyed, the battle progresses across the map, creating a focused, front-line experience.

The Rush that I made for TF2 works exactly the same way, except that since there is no interact button, you must stand near the objective to arm or disarm the objective

Starting early on as I tested how the objectives could work, this was around the time I started using AI to reduce workflow and increase output of what I could do as such within a week I had perfected a type of system that would be playable, and play like Rush where you'd need to arm and defuse the objective

During the construction of the gamemode with the help of my Artist, I personally reached out to voice actors to voice the gamemode, concept artists that could envision the character I had made for the announcer, modellers who could create the assets needed and later on particle makers to make particles for the gamemode.
And due to that, I was able to reach most of the goals I had in mind for creating Rush for TF2

Having made the gamemode, I now had to present it in a way where it could gain interest, and so within a bit of a time I made a map for it

"FLOWERPOT"

2025

Flowerpot was developed in just under two months but was delayed slightly by two weeks since I had to take surgery at the time, which rendered me unable to finish the map with the gamemode in it. It is quite a small map, designed in such a way that each pathway would lead to these objectives that are quite placed linear. Destroying one over the other objective would always lead to an advantage in a different way

Set around a dust valley, with what is supposed to be the behind the Valve map "Granary", it follows as the character "Wendel" plots both of the teams to fight over a piece of land, to destroy Computer stations that are laid across the map, that sends power signals to the objectives that are in the regular maps. Destroying them would render them inoperable, which is why the character tasked the other team to defend them

Rush & Flowerpot released to relatively positive reviews about its new type of gameplay, its graphical style, but was criticized due to its similarity between Control Points due its similar gameplay and HUD Layout

While not having a sense of control points, as you destroy the objectives by planting bombs on them, and the other way around by defusing them, players quickly took quick decisions on the gamemode by primarily looking at the difference between the HUD and the one I made for Rush

The gamemode was primarily made so that mappers/devs could experiment on creating maps that would contain destructible environments

However due to time constraints and pressure to release the map, I had to scrap the idea of having a destructible environment for Source maps, and would only hope so anyone could follow up on that original idea

After the creation of the map, I would be reached out by one of the leaders of a project aiming to build a theme around maps/cosmetics/art and more, that asked if they could include the gamemode, and have me create a map and establish a team that could make more said maps for the project in the Rush mode. I gladly accepted that request

That would lead me to create the second Rush map, that you can read about in the Low Profile Collection Tab under MODS/PROJECTS

"HOLT"

2026

Sometime after the second Rush map "Monoceros" was announced in early 2026, I was reached out by another project leader for another project called "The Northwood Project" that would end later that same year. They gave me an unlimited amount of time for the map, but I felt that I needed to improve the gamemode a bit more, so I started working on it right away

After some delays and some redesigning, Holt was released after 3 months in development, sporting a style that would match the project's goal

Holt is set in alpine environment somewhere in Europe between some mountains, in an important fuel depot, where the story similarly unfolds like Flowerpot, with Wendel tasking the teams to destroy the Computer Stations

Holt released to relatively positive reviews about its new type of gameplay and its graphical style, but was criticized due to its Scale compared to other maps. At this point people had warmed up more to the gamemode, which helped the map's reputation a bit better, as people wasn't complaining so much about the gamemode anymore

Being the last Rush map, it was also the last Source map I would produce before I quit the Source Engine to pursue a more intensive game development role

"POST RELEASE"

2026

Post release of the gamemode I continued to develop on the gamemode vigorously by doing a lot of playtesting, and taking a lot of feedback from various amounts of sources. A lot of the issues of the gamemode, such as loose voice lines, visual indication and gameplay phasing were vital to fix in order to get the message of the gamemode done correctly. It was only until Holt in 2026 where I perfected the gamemode

As the gamemode grew I would continue non development as a mentor, helping any future mappers that took an interest in the map, by solving bugs that they couldn't solve themselves

Check the Low Profile Collection Tab under MODS/PROJECTS to see what maps I mentored prior to quitting Source
