"RED DUSK"

2024 - 2025

Red Dusk is a total overhaul mod for the game Hearts of Iron 4, set in an alternative reality world where the August Coup in the Soviet Union succeeded

The game starts on the first of January 2000, and because of the existence of the Soviet Union, some dictatorships in the world that would have gone away by that time still exists

I started on Red Dusk as a coder, by implementing Red Dusk their new color scheme, sitting and painstakingly setting each countries color, then once I had completed that, I made the mods map font and map style that would over time become iconic. After having done that I started to implement a lot of guidelines for the mod on how flavoured it should be, by fixating at adding small details to the mod, updating leaders to be historically accurate and adding more localization flavour

When developing on Red Dusk, the team and I extensively used GitHub with pull requests and branches to coordinate between ourselves to elevate the workflow between the team's members

About a month into the mod I was also elevated to a "Directing Coder", which is essentially a coder with more elevated freedom to do things their way

At the time around when I joined, they were working on an update that would eventually become the "Across the Atlantic Update", where I coded a few political branches of the UK, a cut path for the United States, Civil War events in India and Pakistan, Civil war intervention mechanics and much much more

"ACROSS THE ATLANTIC - UPDATE"

2025

Across the Atlantic released with quite favourable reviews by both the players who played it and YouTubers who commercialized it

The update released with the new map style, the focus tree for the United Kingdom and more improvements to the mod's storyline

Due to the low development team we had, we unfortunately had to cut most of the content that was originally planned for the update

After Across the Atlantic, we begun working on the next update to the mod, where I worked on Iran's content paths, China's content paths and some new game mechanics.

Iran was going to have a few paths, where it would either devolve into a civil war or continue down its current path. One for the current government, one for a Communist regime and one for either a Shaist or Democratic path. I worked on both extensively for a couple of months with another developer, while doing other tasks on the mod.
China was going to have something similar, with it mostly revolving around the CCP, but that it could also fall into civil war if it lost a war with Taiwan. I only coded the Warlords that would come from the Chinese Civil War.

Around that time I begun complaining about the mod's lack of development phase, which lead me to start to create a country path for Norway.

The game for Norway was going to start historically accurate to its time, where you'd first had to go down a tree about what was happening, before the 2001 election that would decide the fate of the country

Norway was going to have the ability to pick between all the modern political parties, some abstract parties, the Communists and even a made up Fascist party. Norway had a few paths to take outside of the parties, between building the country, strengthening NATO or going its own path, such as rebuilding the Kalmar Union under Norwegian rule. Norway could even fall into civil war, where all the counties would be fighting against you in the Oslo commune

However the content was never completed as I quit the team a few days after getting a heart surgery, and due to some other factors like team tension and lack of progress and team motivation. The content may never see the light of day as the original team was motivated behind a political reason, and wished to focus more on the story rather than the gameplay.
