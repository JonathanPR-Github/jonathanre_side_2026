"LEFT 4 DEAD MOD COLLECTION"

2025

Being a vivid enjoyer of Left 4 Dead 2, it was bound to be upon that i would eventually create something for the game. There was always something bugging me off with it weapons in the game, with how L4D1 had an unique pistol unlike the one in L4D2. I am also one who genuierly enjoys a firstperson model to be below the arm, as it frees up more space to look around

So in early 2025, i sat down and learnt how the weapons worked in L4D2, and with them being so similar to how they worked in TF2, i was able to produce the mod pretty quickly. Since the model already existed in the game, all i needed to do was to swap out the default model with this one, and match rig the animations, then to just do a placement of the origin so the weapon was put more beneath the player

"Minmode - Glock" released to positive feedback, as it seemed people quite enjoyed the simple mod. Shortly after i finalised a request and followed up with a similar mod for another animation mod
