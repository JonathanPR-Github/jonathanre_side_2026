"SWBF2 (2017) COLLECTION"

2025

Star Wars Battlefront II is a 2017 action shooter video game developed by DICE and published by Electronic Arts. It is based on the Star Wars franchise and is the fourth main installment of the Star Wars: Battlefront series and a sequel to the 2015 reboot of the series.

Sometime in late 2025 after having played the game extensively by returning to it, I had an idea for a mod that was both risky and rewarding. Mods for games made by EA, are unfortunately risky, as they all carry the potential to be banned by using them, so I was initially cautious

So in later on from that, I decided to learn how to make such mods for EA games, and that required me to learn the Frosty Modding Tools. By my own words, I can describe the experience with it as quite confusing and not so particularly friendly towards newer users

It would take me a few days of studying the engine, and having to ask around for similar mods to eventually gain a schematic that I could work with, but once I was able to figure it out I was able to produce something neat

So in late that week, I was able to produce the mod "Simple Crosshairs" by grave digging the program within its systems, replacing an asset to display over another, that gives a choice between a variant amount of custom crosshairs from both me and from other games that can be used to be played with

The mod was met with positive reviews, as people had wished for a mod that could give them the choice of some custom crosshairs

In the following months I would be reached by various amounts of devs who similarly wanted to recreated the mod I had made, I gladly obliged on that part, and ever since there should have been a few submods of this mod that also gives custom crosshairs
