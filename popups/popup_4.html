"THE DEATHRUN SAGA COLLECTION"

2018 - 2025

The Deathrun Saga Collection was a collection of 3 Deathrun maps for "Team Fortress 2" developed within the span of 2024 and 2025

Deathrun is a community created game mode in which where one player controls traps in an obstacle course while the rest of the players attempt to survive and reach the end of the map. It emphasizes timing, player interaction, and asymmetrical gameplay design, with adventure designing heavily emphasised.

Starting right after I finished my first attempts at making maps in 2018, I felt at the time like I wanted to try to create something that wasn't going to try to follow the official modes, but that I wanted to expand to the custom modes made by the player base. It would still take me some time to go back to the Hammer as I was going back and forth with school, and with that I paused the map for a whole two years before continuing on it

But around 2020 I finally came around to finish what would become my first proper map with its alpha. After finishing it I continued to develop on it over the next 3 years, to note this was also around a time where I was simultaneously learning the engine, so it would naturally also take a bit of time for me to work things around

During the maps development, I learnt the value of teamwork even as I was making it myself, when I was helped by various people internationally that was similarly also making Deathrun maps, in which I could also lend help by teaching people things they didn't know. This was also when I learnt the value of playtesting and taking feedback, as we playtested my map every time it had a new addition to it, I would receive valuable negative and positive feedback that would help me shape how I could improve my projects for the best

Due to something I can only describe as a Dilemma, I had a constant worry that I had to perfect the map as much as possible, which is also why it took so long to develop. This is where I also developed a thought process a project should always be developed within a certain timeframe and never to be developed for unnecessary amounts of lengths of time. In the end I had to set my foot down, and decide that the map had to be released, in which in 2024 Dustgrowl was finally released. From there map development would go much faster, as the next dr maps I would release would only take a few weeks

DUSTGROWL

2024

Dustgrowl was designed very special, as all of the other deathrun maps I would create, they would have something unique that would make them stand out, and that included the first proper map I made

It is made unique by that there is two whole course levels in the map, meaning that after playing 3 rounds, or winning the game it switches over the map to an entirely new course with completely different traps to change up the gameplay, and so back to the first course by the same gameplay loop. This map made it stand out between other deathrun maps, besides its obvious higher quality in vision and graphics from other deathrun maps at the time

Dustgrowl released to quite favorable reviews from the playtesters who had played it, as most of the map's player base came from those who tested the map. It was praised for both it looks and gameplay mechanics

TIMBERCROVE

2024

Timbercrove was designed to try to see if an open world for a deathrun map would work, with different builds building upon that idea gradually as I was making the map over the span of 2 months

It is made unique by that the players spawn around a hut which is the main objective around a arena of obstacle courses that are explorable by completing each one of them separately, and then to beat each course, you must complete a puzzle by each end of the areas

Timbercrove released to favorable reviews from the playtesters who had played it. However it was never truly added to any of the remaining servers at the time hosting the gamemode, so it was never truly praised

SPIRALSTOVE

2025

Spiralstove was designed around a "pick your adventure" type of gameplay, with the map building upon allowing players to choose their own deathrun course, with the making of the map over the span of 2 months

It is made unique by that a singular player at the start of the match gets to pick out a course of 3 portals in front of them, that eventually leads them to playing a course with completely unique themes to them

Spiralstove released to favorable reviews from the playtesters who had played it and both the players playing it.

Before I left the Deathrun community with this map, I had 62 mappers and testers sign the map in a favour to send me off to the game dev world, a truly connecting experience between a community
