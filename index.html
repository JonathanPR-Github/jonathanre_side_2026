Hello, Welcome to Jonathan Erik Reppen's Portfolio

ABOUT ME

I'm a self-taught game developer, that takes whatever opportunity is available around places and strives to develop on my own and others sectors to eventually get into the industry

I have for many years been modding several video games, like Star Wars Battlefront, STALKER, and more. But my biggest projects were within Map making for Team Fortress 2, and Mod making for Hearts of Iron 4, where I was involved in community projects and standalone total overhaul mods

Ever since I started to see the potential I had as a game developer since I started all the way back in 2016 when I was 15, I've been actively developing myself to further enhance my chances of getting a job as a game developer, and as a project to one day produce a video game. In that time, I have been plenty of visits to game studios like, 2K Studios, been to interviews with game development studios and developed a mobile game as per a group effort. Even as I had and was recovering from a heart surgery, I continued to develop myself to eventually strike gold whenever I could be hired

One thing I've learned as a developer, is to adapt to all kinds of new technologies that comes your way, and that includes Artificial Intelligence that I use regularly to enhance my coding experiences, and to reduce workflow. The overall usage of it has increased the flow by 80% when I work on projects, and that is a very good saying. I normally also tend to work 50/50 with it, with me coding some of the code, and me guiding the artificial intelligence to do it for me.

CURRENT SITUATION

As of April of 2026, I am currently on the bounds to quit the Source engine and move on to fully learn both Unity and Unreal in a gradual amount of time, with Courses and self learning

With the goal of helping me get better chances to get hired in any kind of circumstance.

The second goal, and the thing I will be normally doing alongside it, is developing a video game. Whether I succeed depends on my own abilities as I learn the engines, and the determination I put into it
